# JISRS Submission Guide

Target: **Journal of the Indian Society of Remote Sensing**  
Official home: https://link.springer.com/journal/12524  
Official instructions: https://link.springer.com/journal/12524/submission-guidelines

## Files

1. `JISRS_Main_Manuscript_Anonymous.docx` — designate as **Manuscript/Main Document**
2. `JISRS_Title_Page.docx` — designate as **Title Page**
3. `JISRS_Supplementary_Information.pdf` — designate as **Supplementary Information / Online Resource 1**
4. `cover_letter.pdf` — designate as **Cover Letter**
5. `highlights.txt` — paste or upload when the system requests Highlights
6. `source_package.zip` — upload only when editable LaTeX source is requested

Do not upload the identified title page as part of the anonymous main manuscript.

## Metadata to paste

**Article type:** Research Article

**Title:**  
Terrain-Conditioned Cross-Attention for Cross-Region Avalanche Debris Mapping with Sentinel-1 SAR

**Running title:**  
Terrain-Conditioned Avalanche Mapping

**Keywords:**  
Sentinel-1 SAR; snow avalanche mapping; semantic segmentation; radar geometry; cross-region evaluation; deep learning

Use the current abstract from `paper-jisrs/main.tex`.

## Double-blind proof check

Before approving the generated proof:

- Search the main manuscript and supplementary file for both author names, university, e-mail addresses, and the GitHub username.
- Confirm that the title page is present as a separate identified file.
- Confirm that references cite prior work in the third person.
- Confirm figures, tables, equations, and references render without missing markers.
- Confirm the Word manuscript remains A4, double-spaced, and continuously line-numbered.

## Declarations

- Funding: none
- Competing interests: none
- Human/animal ethics: not applicable
- Consent: not applicable
- Data: AvalCD DOI is stated in the manuscript
- AI disclosure: included in Methods and Statements and Declarations
- Author contributions: supplied on the separate title page

## Publication route

The journal is hybrid. Select the standard subscription route unless the authors deliberately choose and can fund open access. Confirm the live system displays no mandatory page, colour, submission, or publication fee before final approval.

## Integrity

Do not submit if another venue is actively considering this manuscript. On the actual submission date, add JISRS, the manuscript identifier, and `under-review` status to `tools/submitted_works.json`.
